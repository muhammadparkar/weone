# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Business clients and consumers in Qatar seeking diversified services across real estate, laundry, bakery, jewelry, and transportation. Primary visitors are:
- Individuals looking for premium residential/commercial property services
- Families and businesses needing professional laundry and dry cleaning
- Customers seeking artisan bakery products and custom cakes for events
- Jewelry shoppers for occasion pieces, bridal collections, or custom designs
- Logistics and transport service seekers

Context: Mobile and desktop users browsing on-the-go or researching service providers. They value trust signals, clear service offerings, and professional presentation.

## Product Purpose

WeOne is a premium diversified conglomerate website showcasing five distinct business divisions under one unified brand. The goal is to establish credibility across multiple industries, make each division discoverable, and drive inquiries/contact across all services. Success means users understand the breadth of offerings and feel confident contacting any division.

## Brand Personality

Premium yet approachable. The brand conveys established expertise and trustworthiness while remaining welcoming to individual consumers, not just enterprise clients. Think: the polish of a luxury hotel group, the warmth of a neighborhood business that happens to operate at scale.

Three-word personality: **Established, Versatile, Trusted**

Emotional goals: confidence (in quality), clarity (in offerings), connection (across divisions)

## Anti-references

- **Avoid**: Generic corporate blue/white color schemes typical of conglomerate sites
- **Avoid**: The dark tech-startup aesthetic common in Middle East web design
- **Avoid**: Royal gold-on-white luxury that feels cold or inaccessible
- **Avoid**: Generic stock-photo-heavy corporate templates
- **Avoid**: Overly minimal or austere design that undermines warmth
- **Avoid**: Identical card grids with identical interaction patterns

## Design Principles

1. **Unity through diversity**: Five distinct divisions should feel like one cohesive brand, not five separate mini-sites. Use consistent typography and spacing to unify, allow color variation to distinguish divisions.

2. **Show, don't sell**: Let imagery and service details do the persuasive work. Avoid aggressive CTAs or sales language. Trust is earned through presentation.

3. **Expert confidence**: The site should feel like it knows exactly what it's doing. Clear hierarchy, purposeful animations, no visual clutter. When something is right, you notice how natural it feels.

4. **Division discoverability**: Each division gets its moment to shine. Users should easily navigate to their specific interest without wading through irrelevant content.

5. **Local identity, universal appeal**: Based in Qatar, serving both local and international audiences. Design respects cultural context while maintaining international design standards.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- Full keyboard navigation for all interactive elements
- Clear focus states
- Sufficient color contrast (4.5:1 minimum for text)
- Reduced motion support for users who prefer no animations
- Form labels and error states clearly communicated
- Semantic HTML structure
